I am thrilled to share that our new study, *"Integrated multi-omics profiling uncovers miRNA-guided molecular changes in acute spinal cord injury,"* has just been published in Molecular Therapy: Nucleic Acids (MT-NA)!

### What We Did &amp; Why It Matters

- **Approach:** We applied an integrative multi-omics analysis (miRNA, mRNA, and proteomics) to samples from acute Spinal Cord Injury (SCI).
- **Discovery:** We successfully mapped the dynamic and coordinated molecular changes occurring across gene, miRNA, and protein levels post-injury.
- **Key Finding:** We identified several key miRNAs as central drivers of post-injury molecular reprogramming.
- **Therapeutic Potential:** Crucially, in vitro inhibition of miR-20a promoted neurogenesis-related responses, positioning it as a promising therapeutic target for SCI.

Publishing in MT-NA is a true honor. A heartfelt thanks to all co-authors, collaborators, and everyone who supported this challenging but rewarding journey.

I am excited to continue this work and explore how these findings can be translated into new treatments.

Read the full, open-access article here: [doi:10.1016/j.omtn.2025.102746](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.omtn.2025.102746)
